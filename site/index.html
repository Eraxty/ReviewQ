Waiting time counts from the first ship, like the review dash. When a project is shipped again (after "needs changes", or after the maker unshipped it), only the latest ship date is public. The wait then counts from the earliest ship this site has seen, or is estimated from its review history and journals, and is marked with a +. Its real wait can be a bit longer, so it may sit a little higher in the real queue.
